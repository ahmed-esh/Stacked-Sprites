FROM FLAT TO STACKED

A tiny slice of 3D.

A stacked sprite is a set of flat images drawn one above another. Rotate every slice together and offset each one upward to create the illusion of depth.

01 / Start with a photo

Upload a reference, open Generate slices, and choose a provider and model, and enter that provider’s API key. When running this app locally, local runtimes can work without a key. The model designs aligned pixel-art cross-sections inspired by the object. The app draws those slices onto transparent 2D canvases. No mesh or 3D engine is involved.

02 / Review the slices

Choose resolution, automatic or fixed height, art direction, and an output token limit. Review four stacked angles, geometry checks, individual frames, and assumptions, then use the stack or revise your prompt and generate again. Results are stylized interpretations; resemblance and structural quality vary.

03 / Make it yours

Drag the preview, adjust spacing and view angle, or click a slice to inspect the stack up to that layer. Export a transparent PNG or a horizontal sprite sheet. Existing sprite sheets and simple extrusion still work locally without an API key.

Provider documentation ↗

May help with complex shapes, but can take longer and consume more tokens within your cap, leaving less room for the slice recipe. Enabling it does not raise your limit or add requests. Also applies to scene generation.

Complex animals or furniture may benefit from 12,000–14,000 output tokens for more parts and contour sections. Higher limits can increase cost; 8,000 remains the default. Change this only when you need more detail.

Auto height lets the model choose a layer count in the same pixel units as the footprint (up to 2× slice resolution, capped at 96). Fixed counts override those proportions.

The selected reference is sent to the model. It must support images.

How to describe the structure

Name the parts, their proportions, gaps, and facing direction. Describe what the photo hides. Adapt these examples to your object:

  • Chair: Four separate legs, a broad cushion above them, and a low backrest with gaps between vertical slats.
  • Cat: Four separate paws at the base, an elongated body above the legs, a distinct head, pointed ears, and a curved tail behind it.
  • Car: A body longer than it is wide, four separate wheels, recessed wheel arches, and a low roof. Front points toward negative y.

All results are validated locally. A low token limit may truncate the result. No automatic retries or paid repair calls.
